How it works

Mint the NFT, and the token launches itself

1Create

Deploy an NFT collection for your token — set supply and a free or paid mint.

2Mint

People mint the NFT (1 per wallet). Paid mints fund the token's liquidity.

3Auto-launch

On sold-out, the token deploys, a Uniswap V4 market opens, and liquidity is locked.

4Earn

Minters claim a locked airdrop; trade fees feed the NFT floor and pay the creator.

Why Vesper

Fair, safe, and self-reinforcing

✦Liquidity is burned

The LP is sent to the dead address at launch — it can never be pulled.

✦Airdrop follows the NFT

10% of supply to minters, locked one month; the claim travels with the NFT.

✦Fee-backed floor

30% of trade fees back the NFTs — an active token lifts every holder's floor.

✦Always liquid

Redeem an NFT to its floor anytime after the lock — no buyer needed.
