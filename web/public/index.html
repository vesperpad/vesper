How it works

Mint the NFT, the token launches itself

1Create

Set up an NFT collection for your token. Choose the supply and a free or paid mint.

2Mint

People mint, one per wallet. A paid mint funds the token's liquidity.

3Launch

When the collection sells out, the token goes live on Uniswap and the liquidity is locked.

4Earn

Minters get a locked airdrop, and trade fees feed the NFT floor.

Transparency

Built so you don't have to trust us

✦Liquidity is burned

Every launch sends its LP to the dead address. Nobody, us included, can ever pull it.

✦No premine

Fixed 1B supply: 10% to minters (locked 1 month), 90% into the locked pool. No team bag.

✦Open source

Every contract is public on GitHub. Read it, verify it.

✦Same code for all

One shared factory and fee hook power every token, so there's nothing custom hiding in your launch.
