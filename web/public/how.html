How it works

An NFT mint that launches a token

The collection is a presale, an airdrop ticket, and a fee-backed floor at the same time.

1Create the collection

Set your token details and the NFT collection: supply, and a free or paid mint, one per wallet.

2People mint

Everyone mints at the same price. On a paid mint, half the ETH becomes the token's liquidity and the rest goes to the creator and dev.

3It sells out, the token launches

The token deploys, a Uniswap V4 market opens, and the liquidity is locked. No bonding curve, no waiting.

4The airdrop unlocks

10% of supply goes to minters, locked for a month. The claim stays with the NFT, so whoever holds it claims it.

5Fees feed the floor

Trades pay 2.5% each way. 30% of that fills a vault that backs the NFTs, so the more the token trades, the higher the floor.

6Exit whenever

Sell the NFT, or redeem it to the floor for its share of the vault. Redeeming burns the NFT and any unclaimed airdrop.
